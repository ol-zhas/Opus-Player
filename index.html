Our Core Privacy Commitment: Opus Player processes your voice notes and speech-to-text transcriptions 100% locally on your device. We do NOT collect, upload, store, or transmit your voice notes, transcripts, or personal messages to any cloud servers.

1. Introduction

Nomad Apps ("we", "us", or "our") develops Opus Player (also published as OpusPlayer: WA Audio to Text AI, package name: com.opusplayer.audio.olzhas). This Privacy Policy informs you of our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

By using Opus Player, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. On-Device Voice Notes & Speech-to-Text Processing

Opus Player is engineered with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • Audio Files Access: The app accesses voice note files (such as .opus, .m4a, .ogg) selected by you via the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF). These files remain entirely on your device and are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • 100% Offline AI Transcription: When you transcribe a voice note, speech recognition is executed directly on your smartphone's hardware using an embedded on-device engine (Sherpa-ONNX). Your audio recordings and generated transcripts never leave your device and are never sent across the Internet.
  • Converted Audio & Custom Notes: When converting voice notes to M4A, adding custom titles, tags, or personal notes, all data is saved locally in the app's sandboxed storage on your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Collection

To provide free access, ensure software stability, and handle premium subscriptions, Opus Player integrates verified third-party SDKs that collect technical, pseudonymized data:

A. Google AdMob (Google LLC)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free tier of the app. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Coarse device location and IP address (for localized ad delivery)
  • Ad interaction metrics and diagnostics

Consent & GDPR: Opus Player integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland can review and modify their advertising consent choices at any time via Settings → Privacy choices inside the app.

For more details, review Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

B. Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics (Google LLC)

To detect app crashes, resolve bugs, and monitor reliability across different Android versions, we use Firebase Crashlytics. The data collected includes:

  • Crash stack traces and error logs
  • Device diagnostics (device model, operating system version, available memory)
  • Pseudonymous installation identifiers

For more details, review the Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.

C. In-App Subscriptions & Purchases (RevenueCat & Google Play)

If you purchase Opus Player Pro, payments and recurring subscriptions are handled securely through Google Play In-App Billing and managed via RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.).

  • We do not collect, view, or store your credit card or financial information. All billing transactions are managed directly by Google.
  • RevenueCat securely verifies purchase receipts and manages your Pro entitlements using anonymous, pseudonymous app user IDs.

For more details, review RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4. Android Permissions Used

Opus Player requests only permissions necessary for core functionality:

Permission Purpose
Storage / SAF (Folder Access) Allows you to choose WhatsApp or custom folders to scan and play voice notes locally.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ to display media playback controls (play/pause/seek) in the notification bar.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE (Media Playback) Ensures smooth, continuous audio playback when the app is minimized or the screen is turned off.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required for loading ads (AdMob), reporting crash logs (Crashlytics), and verifying Pro subscriptions via RevenueCat / Google Play.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS (Optional) Ensures audio playback is not unexpectedly killed by system battery optimization features.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Opus Player does not require user accounts or maintain remote servers with your data:

  • All your custom titles, tags, user notes, and favorites are stored exclusively on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data via Android Settings → Apps → Opus Player → Clear Data immediately and permanently deletes all stored preferences, local databases, and cached speech models.

6. Children's Privacy

Opus Player is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take prompt corrective action.

7. Security of Your Data

The security of your personal voice recordings is our highest priority. Your audio files and transcripts are never transmitted over the Internet. All network communications with third-party SDKs (ads, crash analytics, subscription validation) are protected using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
